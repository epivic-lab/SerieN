https://github.com/epivic-lab/SerieN/blob/76bad6ea8f70775349d8e051787535ba21f11ef2/kenney_train-kit.zip

Six GLB models and their shared colormap are extracted without modification.
Runtime transforms adapt their width/length and orientation to the simulation.
These are stylized models, not replicas of named DB locomotives or Kato track.
The kit's track models are not used: track geometry remains the existing Kato reconstruction.
